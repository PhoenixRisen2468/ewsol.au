What I do

Water work. Done properly.

Design, install support, fix and commission. Plain English. Fixed quotes where we can.

01

Centre pivots & irrigation

Texas-style centre pivots and rotating irrigators — pump feed, electrics, filtration and getting the circle turning again when pressure drops at the end tower.

02

Pump stations & boosters

Duty matching, suction that doesn’t cavitate, VFDs, sealing. Sized for your head and flow — not a catalogue guess.

03

Dam, river & bore filtration

Keep grit, algae and sand out of nozzles and pivots so you’re not unblocking lines at 5am.

04

Off-grid & solar water

Bores, tanks, rainwater, solar pumping for lifestyle blocks and shacks. I’ve lived off-grid on solar for about ten years — I know what fails in winter.

05

Water & wastewater infrastructure

Treatment plant mechanical, clarifiers, pipelines, rising mains — the heavy stuff. Track record from mainland utilities and mine sites.

06

Contractor support

Bidding Tassie water or hydro work and need a mechanical/water lead? I turn up, read the drawings, and keep the water side honest.
