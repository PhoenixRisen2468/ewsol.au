About Us

The Person Behind The Work

Mitchell Jacques founded Engineered Water Solutions after more than three decades in mechanical and water engineering — from remote PNG islands to 60-metre tunnels beneath the Brisbane River. Now based in Tasmania, he’s focused on the water systems farmers and growers actually rely on.

As founder and director of Meyjor Industries (2012–2023), Mitchell grew the business from $1M to $12M in a single year while maintaining zero OH&S incidents across every site he's ever run. That same standard applies to farm pump stations, pivot feed systems and irrigation upgrades.

Mitchell brings engineering depth plus commercial and legal rigour — Bachelor of Business (QUT), Bachelor of Arts in Economics (UQ), Master of Business Communication (UoW), and a near-complete Juris Doctor in Commercial Law (ANU). When he signs off on a contract, he understands every word of it — and he’ll explain it in plain English.
